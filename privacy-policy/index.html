Smaran: Mantra Chanting

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Smaran has no accounts and no servers of its own. The points below describe this app specifically and apply in addition to the general policy above.

  • Information stored on your device

    Your settings, your practice history (which mantra, when, how many chants, for how long) and any mantras you add yourself (text, images, audio you choose) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

  • Advertising (free version)

    The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app details, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Smaran Pro removes ads. An optional rewarded video can be watched to unlock report export for a short time.

  • Purchases (Smaran Pro)

    Smaran Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play handles all payment details; we never see or store them. The app only checks with Google Play whether you have an active subscription. Manage or cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

  • Reports and your own files

    When you share or export a report, the app hands it to the app you choose (for example Gmail or Drive); we never receive it. Images and audio for your own mantras are picked through Android’s file picker, and only the files you select are read.

  • Permissions

    Internet and network state: to load ads and check your Pro subscription.
    Notifications (Pro): playback controls and chant count while the screen is off; you can deny this and the app still works.
    Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep audio playing with the screen off (Pro) and to keep the screen on while chanting if you turn that setting on.
    Google Play Billing: to sell and verify the Pro subscription.
    Advertising ID: used by the Google ads SDK to serve and measure ads.
